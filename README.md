# Murmur AI

AI communication copilot for communication anxiety
沟通焦虑场景的 AI 沟通助手

Murmur helps users turn difficult-to-express thoughts into messages they feel safe and confident sending.
Murmur 帮助用户把难以表达的想法，转化为自己真正愿意、也有信心发送的消息。

## Overview

Murmur is designed for moments when users know what they want to say, but become stuck because of emotional or interpersonal uncertainty.
Murmur 面向这样的沟通时刻：用户知道自己想说什么，却因为情绪或人际关系中的不确定性而卡住。

It is not simply an AI writing tool. It supports users in understanding the communication context, forming an appropriate response, deciding whether it feels right to send, and taking action.
它不是单纯的 AI 写作工具，而是帮助用户理解沟通场景、形成合适的表达、判断自己是否愿意发送，并最终采取行动。

## Product Insight

Users are not necessarily unable to communicate. In high emotional-risk moments, they often lose confidence in their own judgment.
用户不一定是不会沟通，而是在高情感风险时刻失去了对自己判断的信心。

Murmur therefore focuses not only on what to say, but also on why it is okay to say it.
因此，Murmur 关注的不只是“应该说什么”，也关注“为什么这样说是可以的”。

The goal is to help users communicate with greater clarity and confidence without replacing their own judgment.
Murmur 的目标是在不替用户做决定的情况下，帮助他们更清晰、更有信心地表达自己。

## Product Philosophy

* Help users say what they genuinely want to say.
    帮助用户表达自己真正想说的话。
* Support communication rather than people-pleasing.
    支持真实沟通，而不是迎合他人。
* Preserve the user’s own voice rather than optimizing for what the other person wants to hear.
    保留用户自己的表达方式，而不是迎合对方想听的话。
* Murmur is not a communication decision-maker, therapist, or reminder tool.
    Murmur 不是沟通决策代理、心理治疗工具，也不是提醒工具。
