# User Interview 02

## Participant｜访谈对象

| Field 字段 | Details 内容 |
|---|---|
| Age 年龄 | 26 |
| Role 身份 | Elementary school teacher / 小学教师 |
| Main Scenario 主要场景 | Workplace & parent communication / 职场与家校沟通 |
| Communication Issue 沟通问题 | Communication fatigue / 沟通精力消耗 |

## Concrete Events｜具体事件

### Event 01

The user needed to message a student's parent after noticing a decline in the student's performance. She wanted to communicate the concern and suggest reducing the student's phone use and increasing parental involvement.

用户发现学生学习表现下降后，需要给家长发消息。她希望表达自己的担忧，同时建议家长控制手机使用并增加陪伴。

After a busy day, she gave the core idea to Doubao and asked it to generate a complete message. She selected the version closest to her communication style, edited the tone and form of address, and sent it.

忙碌一天后，她把核心想法告诉豆包，让 AI 生成完整消息。随后选择最符合自己表达习惯的版本，修改语气和称呼后发送。

## Existing Workarounds｜当前解决方式

- Ask friends or colleagues for help with messages to leaders / 找朋友或同事帮忙处理领导相关沟通
- Use Doubao for school-parent communication / 使用豆包处理家校沟通
- Search Xiaohongshu for school notice templates / 在小红书搜索学校通知模板

The user chooses different tools depending on the communication context and her available energy.

用户会根据沟通场景和当时的精力状态选择不同的解决方式。

## Product Concept Reaction｜产品概念反馈

The user said she would use Murmur, but initially perceived it as similar to Doubao.

用户表示愿意使用 Murmur，但最初认为它与豆包的功能比较相似。

She felt that a dedicated communication tool would need additional value beyond general AI generation to justify being a separate product.

她认为，如果只是专门用于沟通的 AI，它需要提供通用 AI 没有的额外价值，才能成为一个独立产品。

She also showed interest in a persistent communication profile that could maintain different communication styles, such as communicating with parents or clients.

她对能够长期记录不同沟通对象和沟通风格的功能表现出兴趣，例如针对家长、客户等不同关系形成不同的沟通方式。

## Hypothesis Validation｜假设验证

| Hypothesis 假设 | Result 结果 | Evidence 证据 |
|---|---|---|
| H1. Users may seek help in high-anxiety communication situations. / 用户会在高焦虑沟通场景寻求帮助 | Partially supported / 部分支持 | The user seeks help when communication is tiring or she does not want to spend additional energy. / 用户在沟通成本较高、精力不足时会寻求帮助 |
| H2. Lower input threshold may increase usage. / 更低的输入门槛可能提高使用意愿 | Not sufficiently tested / 验证不足 | The input-threshold question was not fully answered. / 本次访谈没有充分验证输入门槛 |
| H3. Users may accept AI communication assistance. / 用户可能接受 AI 沟通辅助 | Supported / 支持 | The user already uses AI to generate communication messages and edits the output before sending. / 用户已经使用 AI 生成沟通内容，并在修改后发送 |
| H4. Post-send reassurance may be valuable. / 发送后的确认可能有价值 | Not sufficiently tested / 验证不足 | Post-send reassurance was not a major issue in this interview. / 发送后的确认并不是本次访谈的主要问题 |
| H5. Users may be willing to pay for dedicated communication support. / 用户可能愿意为专门的沟通支持付费 | Early signal only / 初步信号 | The user indicated a willingness to pay around ¥10/month. / 用户表达了约10元/月的付费意愿 |

## Key Findings｜核心发现

1. **General AI is already a viable workaround.**  
   **通用 AI 已经能够解决一部分真实沟通需求。**

2. **Communication need does not automatically mean independent-product need.**  
   **用户有沟通需求，不等于一定需要一个独立的沟通 AI 产品。**

3. **Privacy and energy cost affect the choice between friends and AI.**  
   **隐私和沟通精力成本会影响用户在朋友与 AI 之间的选择。**

4. **Persistent communication profiles may provide additional value.**  
   **长期沟通档案可能成为区别于通用 AI 的额外价值。**

## Product Implications｜产品影响

- Murmur needs differentiation beyond message generation.  
  Murmur 需要提供超越“生成一条消息”的差异化价值。

- Communication profiles should make their effect on outputs visible to the user.  
  沟通档案需要让用户明显感受到它如何影响 AI 的输出。

- Compare Murmur against general AI as an existing workaround, rather than treating users as if they have no current solution.  
  应把通用 AI 视为真实的现有替代方案，而不是假设用户目前没有解决方式。
