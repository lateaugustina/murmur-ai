# User Interview 01

## Participant｜访谈对象

| Field 字段 | Details 内容 |
|---|---|
| Age 年龄 | 19 |
| Role 身份 | Undergraduate student / 本科生 |
| Main Scenario 主要场景 | Intimate relationship / 亲密关系 |
| Communication Issue 沟通问题 | Relationship uncertainty / 关系不确定 |

## Concrete Events｜具体事件

### Event 01

Online friend of four years. The user mistakenly thought her friend was about to take the Gaokao and sent early wishes. The friend clarified that she was only in her second year of high school.

用户与一位认识四年的网友聊天时，误以为对方即将参加高考，于是提前送上祝福。对方解释自己其实才高二。

The user felt guilty and awkward, then explained the misunderstanding and sent a sticker. She waited about six minutes before replying and felt relieved afterward.

用户感到内疚和尴尬，随后解释自己记错了，并发送了一个表情。她大约犹豫了六分钟后回复，发送后明显松了一口气。

### Event 02

The user was asked why she rarely shared her daily life with someone. She felt that directly saying “we are not close enough” would be too harsh, so she sent a screenshot to a friend for confirmation.

用户曾被问到为什么很少分享自己的日常。她觉得直接说“我们还没有熟到那个程度”太直接，于是把聊天截图发给朋友确认。

After the friend agreed with her interpretation, she felt confident enough to send the reply.

朋友认可她的判断后，她才更有信心发送回复。

## Existing Workarounds｜当前解决方式

- Ask friends to review messages / 找朋友帮忙看消息
- Ask friends to interpret the other person's intention / 找朋友分析对方意图
- Use Doubao for communication analysis / 使用豆包分析沟通情境
- Use AI mainly for interpretation rather than direct message generation / 更倾向让 AI 帮忙分析，而不是直接代写

## Product Concept Reaction｜产品概念反馈

The user showed interest in a dedicated communication tool, especially for situations where she does not want to ask friends or feels that friends may not fully understand the context.

用户对专门用于沟通的工具有兴趣，尤其是在不方便问朋友，或者觉得朋友未必能完全理解情境的时候。

She also suggested relationship profiles and context-specific support, such as remembering the communication style of different people.

她还提出了关系档案、不同关系对象的沟通风格记录等想法。

## Hypothesis Validation｜假设验证

| Hypothesis 假设 | Result 结果 | Evidence 证据 |
|---|---|---|
| H1. Users may seek help in high-anxiety communication situations. / 用户会在高焦虑沟通场景寻求帮助 | Partially supported / 部分支持 | The user sought confirmation when she felt uncertain about how to respond. / 用户在不确定如何回应时会寻求确认 |
| H2. Lower input threshold may increase usage. / 更低的输入门槛可能提高使用意愿 | Not sufficiently tested / 验证不足 | The interview did not directly test this. / 本次访谈没有直接验证 |
| H3. Users may accept AI communication assistance. / 用户可能接受 AI 沟通辅助 | Supported with conditions / 有条件支持 | The user already used AI for communication analysis, but preferred to retain her own judgment. / 用户已经使用 AI 分析沟通，但仍希望保留自己的判断 |
| H4. Post-send reassurance may be valuable. / 发送后的确认可能有价值 | Not supported in this case / 本次未支持 | The user felt relieved after sending and did not report prolonged post-send anxiety. / 用户发送后很快释然，没有明显持续的发送后焦虑 |
| H5. Users may be willing to pay for dedicated communication support. / 用户可能愿意为专门的沟通支持付费 | Early signal only / 初步信号 | The user indicated a willingness to pay up to ¥30/month. / 用户表示月付意愿最高约为30元 |

## Key Findings｜核心发现

1. **Privacy can be a reason to choose AI over friends.**  
   **隐私可以成为用户选择 AI 而不是朋友的原因。**

2. **Users may need judgment confirmation rather than pure message generation.**  
   **用户需要的可能不只是代写，而是确认自己的判断是否合理。**

3. **Dedicated communication context may create value beyond general AI.**  
   **专门的沟通场景和上下文可能成为区别于通用 AI 的价值来源。**

## Product Implications｜产品影响

- Explore a private, dedicated communication environment.  
  探索更私密、专门的沟通环境。

- Preserve user judgment rather than fully replacing it with AI-generated replies.  
  保留用户自己的判断，而不是让 AI 完全代替用户做决定。

- Further validate whether relationship profiles and persistent context provide meaningful differentiation from general AI.  
  继续验证关系档案和长期上下文是否能形成区别于通用 AI 的实际价值。
